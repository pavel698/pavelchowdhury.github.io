About Me

My academic and research interests focus on understanding the behavior of advanced materials and developing engineering solutions through experimental and computational approaches.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering at Utah State University. My research interests include composite materials, polymer-based materials, soft materials, computational mechanics, and materials for aerospace applications.

My previous research experience includes polymer matrix composites, nanoparticle dispersion, mechanical testing, and materials characterization using techniques such as scanning electron microscopy and FTIR analysis.

I am particularly interested in combining experimental research with computational methods, including molecular dynamics and finite element analysis, to better understand material behavior across multiple length scales.
